2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information. Your email address and a password (hashed, never stored in plain text). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier, your email address (or Apple’s private relay address, if you choose to hide it), and the name you elect to share. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile information. Username, display name, profile picture, and any social handles (Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Facebook) you choose to add.
  • Fitness and body information you enter. Gender, age, height, weight, training frequency, goals, and the workouts, exercises, sets, repetitions and weights you log. In some jurisdictions this may be treated as health-related or sensitive information. You choose whether to provide it.
  • Content you submit for scanning. Workout video files or photos you upload or record, and links to third-party videos (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) that you paste.
  • Community content. Routines you publish, comments, votes, follows, reports you file, and any message you send us.
  • Support correspondence. Anything you send to support@repai.lol.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage events. Product events such as sign-up, sign-in, a scan succeeding or failing, a routine being saved, a workout being logged, and subscription events, each recorded with your account identifier and a timestamp so we can operate and improve the Service.
  • Device and technical data. Device type and operating system, app version, IP address (processed by our hosting and security providers, including for abuse prevention and rate limiting), and crash or error information.
  • Push notification token. If you enable notifications, a device push token so we can deliver them. You can revoke this in your device settings at any time.
  • Advertising identifier. If you use the free tier, our advertising partner may access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) as described in Section 8. Where Apple requires it, we will request your permission through the App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking occurs on iOS, and we honour your choice.
  • Usage limits. Counts of scans performed in a period, so we can enforce free-tier limits and rate limits.

2.3 Information from third parties

  • Purchase information. When you subscribe, Apple or Google processes your payment and our subscription provider tells us whether your subscription is active and when it renews or expires. We never receive your full payment card number.
  • Publicly available video content. When you paste a link, we retrieve the video at that URL in order to analyse it, along with basic metadata such as the thumbnail.
We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined under California law. See Section 12.

5. AI processing of your content

RepAI uses third-party artificial intelligence services to read your workout content:

  • Video, photo and text analysis. When you submit a video, a photo of a written workout, or a link, the media (or the frames and on-screen text within it) is transmitted to Google’s Gemini API for analysis, and the structured result is returned to you.
  • Profile picture moderation. Profile pictures are sent to Google Cloud Vision for automated safety classification before they are published, and may additionally be assessed against our content rules.

Uploaded media is deleted from our storage immediately after analysis completes. We retain the structured result (the exercises, sets and repetitions), a content hash used to avoid re-processing an identical file, and, where applicable, a thumbnail. Our AI providers process the content on our behalf under their enterprise terms and are not permitted to use it to train their models.

AI output can be wrong. It is a starting point you should review and edit, not a substitute for professional instruction. See Section 8 of our Terms of Service.

8. Advertising and analytics

Free-tier users are shown advertisements supplied by Google AdMob, including rewarded advertisements you may choose to watch in exchange for a scan. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier and information about your interaction with ads. On iOS, tracking is not permitted unless you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; on Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. You can also remove advertising entirely by subscribing to RepAI Pro. Google’s handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Our product analytics are first-party: events are stored in our own database and keyed to your account. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs that build advertising profiles about you.

9. Information you make public

The following are visible to other users, and in some cases to anyone on the internet:

  • Your username, display name, profile picture, any social handles you add, your rank, and your public statistics.
  • Routines you choose to publish, and the comments and votes you leave on public routines.
  • Your position on leaderboards.

Do not publish anything you would not want to be public. You can delete public routines and comments at any time, but copies may persist in caches or in other users’ saved copies.
