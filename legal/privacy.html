Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

RepAI (“RepAI,” “we,” “us”) helps you turn workout videos into structured routines. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and the credentials used to sign in.
  • Profile details you provide — such as display name, username, gender, age, height, weight, and training goals.
  • Content you submit — workout videos, images, or links you provide for analysis, and the routines, workouts, and notes you save.
  • Usage data — basic in-app events (e.g. a scan succeeded or a workout was logged) used to operate and improve the service.

How we use your information

We use your information to provide the core service — detecting exercises from your videos, building and saving routines, tracking your workout history, and powering optional community features (public routines, comments, follows). We also use it to keep the service secure and to fix and improve features.

How videos and images are used

Media you submit is sent to our AI provider to detect exercises and is deleted from our processing storage immediately after analysis. We do not retain your raw video after a scan completes.

Data storage and security

Your account, profile, and routines are stored with our backend provider (Supabase) and protected by row-level security so that, by default, only you can access your private data. Content you explicitly choose to make public (e.g. a routine you publish to the community) is visible to other users.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that run RepAI on our behalf (such as our hosting, database, push-notification, and AI providers), and where required by law.

Subscriptions

Optional RepAI Pro subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play and by our subscription provider (RevenueCat). Payment details are handled by those platforms, not by us; we receive only your subscription status.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, delete saved routines, and permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account, which removes your data from our systems.

Children

RepAI is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@repai.lol.
