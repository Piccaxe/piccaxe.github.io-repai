Common questions

How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your data.

How do I manage my subscription? Subscriptions are managed in your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings. In the app, see Settings → Manage subscription.

A scan looks wrong. Use the “Bad scan?” option on the result screen to send feedback, or rescan with a clearer video.
